About me

Hi, I’m Megan (Minying Qiu), currently pursuing my M.A. in Learning Technology and Experience Design (LTXD) at NYU.

I’ve always been passionate about art. I started with painting and digital art in my childhood. Before NYU, I earned a BFA in Entertainment Design from ArtCenter College of Design in Pasadena, California, where I learned to craft stories and bring characters to life through design.

Now, I’m exploring a new area of interaction, logic, and psychology, aiming to blend my creative skills with user experience, visual, and technology.

Besides design, I also love singing, gaming, and traveling.
